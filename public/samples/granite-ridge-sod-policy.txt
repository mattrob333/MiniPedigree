Granite Ridge Distribution — Segregation of Duties Policy (excerpt for agent grounding)

Scope: applies to all humans AND AI agents acting on ERP (SAP S/4HANA), WMS,
CRM, and identity systems. An agent inherits its owner's SOD constraints and
may never combine duties its owner is prohibited from combining.

PROHIBITED DUTY COMBINATIONS (no single person or agent may hold both sides):

SOD-01  Vendor master data maintenance  ×  Invoice/payment approval
        (creating or changing vendor records, incl. bank details, must be
        separate from approving invoices or releasing payment runs)

SOD-02  Purchase order creation/approval  ×  Goods receipt posting
        (whoever orders may not also confirm receipt)

SOD-03  Goods receipt posting  ×  Invoice processing (3-way match execution)

SOD-04  Customer credit limit changes  ×  Sales order entry or release of
        blocked orders for the same account

SOD-05  Price file / discount maintenance  ×  Customer credit or rebate
        approval

SOD-06  ERP role provisioning  ×  Approval of one's own access, or access
        certification of accounts one provisioned

SOD-07  Journal entry creation  ×  Journal entry approval / period close

SOD-08  RMA / credit memo creation  ×  Credit memo approval above $1,000

APPROVAL THRESHOLDS:
- Payment runs: AP Manager approval required; > $250,000 requires Controller.
- Customer discounts beyond list: Branch Manager to 5%; VP Sales above 5%.
- Vendor bank detail changes: callback verification + Controller approval.
- New ERP roles: line manager + IT Security & Access Manager approval;
  quarterly user access reviews are mandatory and evidence must be retained.

AGENT-SPECIFIC RULES:
- Agents run read-only or draft-only by default. Any write to a system of
  record requires a named human approver and an audit event.
- An agent detecting an SOD conflict must stop and escalate to the Chief
  Compliance Officer (Elena Vasquez); it must not proceed "just this once."
- All agent actions must carry the manifest trace_id and be retained 90 days
  minimum (7 years for financial-close artifacts).
